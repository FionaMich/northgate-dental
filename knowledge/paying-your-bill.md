## Ways to pay
You can pay in the app with a secure payment link, at the front desk, or by phone. We accept debit and credit cards, and HSA and FSA cards. We do not accept cash at Back Bay.

## When payment is due
Your share is due on the day of the visit. When insurance pays less than estimated, we send you a statement for the difference.

## Payment plans
For treatment over $500 you can spread the cost over up to 6 monthly payments with no interest. Ask the team before treatment starts; they set it up with you.

## Receipts
A receipt is emailed after every payment and is also kept in the app. You can ask the team for an itemised statement for your insurance or tax records.
