## What they are for
A video consultation is a 20-minute call with a Northgate dentist for advice, a second opinion, a follow-up after treatment, or to decide whether you need to come in. It is not for emergencies.

## Booking and paying
Book it like any visit, in the app or with Nora: choose Video consultation. It is paid in advance with a secure link, and you can cancel up to 24 hours before for a full refund.

## Joining
Open Visits in the app. The Join button appears on the visit 10 minutes before it starts. Use a quiet, private place with good light, and allow the app to use your camera and microphone.

## Privacy
The call goes directly between you and the dentist. We do not record video consultations.
