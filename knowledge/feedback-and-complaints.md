## Telling us how we did
After each visit we may ask for a short rating in the app. It goes to the clinic manager, never to a public website without your permission.

## Making a complaint
Tell any member of the team, or ask Nora to pass it on. The practice manager replies within 5 working days and tells you what we will change.
