## What to bring
Bring a photo ID, your dental insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medicines you take. If your insurance card is already in the app under Record, you do not need the paper card.

## When to arrive
Arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit so we can check your details, and 5 minutes early after that.

## Forms
New patients fill in their health history in the app before the first visit. It takes about 10 minutes and saves time at the front desk.

## Anxiety and comfort
Tell us if you feel nervous about dental visits. We can explain each step before we do it, agree a stop signal, and book a longer visit so nothing feels rushed.
