## How much notice we need
Please cancel or move a visit at least 24 hours before it starts. You can do it yourself in the app under Visits, with the link in your reminder email, or by asking Nora.

## Missed visits
A visit missed without 24 hours notice has a missed-visit fee of $50 at Back Bay and Cambridge. Quincy does not charge a missed-visit fee. We waive the fee once a year, and always for illness, a family emergency or bad weather that closes roads.

## Running late
If you will be more than 15 minutes late, let us know. We will see you if the dentist's next patient is not delayed, or move your visit to the first free time.

## When we cancel
If we have to cancel, for example because a dentist is ill, we tell you by email and in the app as early as we can and offer the first free times. There is never a fee when we cancel.
