## Ordering
Order dental care products in the Shop tab of the app. Most orders are ready to collect at your clinic within 2 working days.

## Refills
Products such as aligner cleaning tablets and replacement brush heads can be set as a refill, so a new order is made every few months. You can pause or stop a refill in the app at any time.

## Returns
Unopened products can be returned within 30 days for a refund. For hygiene reasons we cannot take back opened products unless they are faulty.
