## Copies of your records
You can see your visits, treatment plans and shared documents in the app. For a full copy of your dental record, ask the team; we send it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

## Correcting your record
If something in your record is wrong, tell the team. We correct it or add your note to the record.

## Who sees your information
Only Northgate staff who need it for your care, billing or your request can see your record. Nora, the AI receptionist, sees only what she needs for the request you make, and never shares one patient's information with another.

## Sharing with others
We share your information with your insurer for claims, and with another clinician only with your permission.
