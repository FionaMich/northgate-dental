## Plans we work with
We bill many dental plans directly. The app lists the plans we are in network with when you add your coverage card. If your plan is not listed, we can still see you and give you a claim form to send to your insurer.

## Adding your insurance
Take a photo of the front and back of your card in the app under Record. The team checks it before your visit and tells you if anything is missing.

## Estimates
Before larger treatment we give you a written estimate of what insurance should pay and what you will pay. It is an estimate: the insurer decides the final amount.

## Pre-authorisation
For crowns, implants, orthodontics and other major treatment, many plans want a pre-authorisation first. We send it for you; it usually takes 2 to 4 weeks.
