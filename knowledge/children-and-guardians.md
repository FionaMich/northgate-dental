## A child's first visit
We recommend a first visit by a child's first birthday, or within 6 months of the first tooth.

## Guardians
Patients under 18 need a parent or legal guardian to agree to treatment. A guardian can manage a child's visits in their own app: ask the team to link the child's record to your account.

## At the visit
A parent or guardian stays with children under 12. Older children may be seen alone if they and their guardian prefer.
