# X-ray images used as synthetic practice documents

Real, de-identified dental radiographs published on Wikimedia Commons under open licences. They are shared with
synthetic patients in the demo only; they belong to no Northgate patient. Each was converted to greyscale JPEG and
re-encoded, which removes embedded metadata; `panoramic-adult.jpg` was also cropped to remove machine text.
Under the share-alike licences these derivatives carry the same licence as their source.
